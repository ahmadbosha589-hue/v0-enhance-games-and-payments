## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days if:
- You haven't deployed to production
- You haven't made major modifications
- There's a genuine technical issue

No refund if:
- You changed your mind
- You lack technical skills
- Third-party services have issues

### What happens when my updates expire?

**Standard License (after 6 months):**
- You keep your current version forever
- You can continue using it commercially
- You won't receive new features or security updates
- You can purchase update extensions

**Extended License:**
- Lifetime updates included
- No expiration

### Can I transfer my license?

**Standard License:** No, non-transferable
**Extended License:** Yes, one transfer allowed (contact support)

### What if I violate the license?

- License terminates automatically
- You must cease all use immediately
- You must destroy all copies
- Legal action may be taken

### Do I need to show the license to my users?

No, end users don't need to see the license. It's between you (developer) and the author.

### Can my developer/contractor use my license?

Yes, your developer can work with the code under YOUR license. They cannot:
- Keep copies after the project
- Use it for other projects
- Transfer or share it

- SaaS rights (recurring revenue potential)
- Lifetime updates (years of value)
