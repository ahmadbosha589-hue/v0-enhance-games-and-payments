# Licensing and Asset Transfer

The repository's old Standard/Extended `$499/$999` licensing model has been retired for the planned one-time sale. The current package is documented as an exclusive source-code asset transfer, subject to a signed bill of sale and the terms in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

## Current sale model

- Exclusive transfer applies only to Seller-owned custom material expressly identified in the bill of sale.
- The buyer may modify, deploy, commercialize, sublicense, assign, and resell the acquired Seller-owned asset.
- Third-party dependencies, providers, accounts, domains, trademarks, user data, and secrets are excluded unless separately listed.
- The repository was public before being changed to private, so historical third-party copies cannot be guaranteed erased.
- The buyer must create its own provider accounts and credentials.

## Files

- [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE): draft transfer agreement and signatures.
- [`LICENSE-BASIC.md`](../LICENSE-BASIC.md): plain-language buyer summary.
- [`LICENSE-EXTENDED.md`](../LICENSE-EXTENDED.md): commercial rights and handover details.

## Before accepting payment

1. Identify the exact repository, commit, domain, brand, and other included assets.
2. State the purchase price and any support/handover period in a bill of sale.
3. Confirm that the Seller owns or is authorized to transfer the listed material.
4. Have a qualified lawyer review the agreement and jurisdiction.
5. Never transfer existing secrets or live user data.
6. Rotate/revoke project credentials and have the buyer create replacements.

This documentation is not legal advice and does not replace a signed agreement.
